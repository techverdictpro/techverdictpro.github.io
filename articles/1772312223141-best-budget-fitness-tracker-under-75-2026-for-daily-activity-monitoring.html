Focus on specific features you'll actually use rather than comprehensive sensor arrays that add complexity without practical benefit.

However, avoid pressing buttons underwater and rinse with fresh water after pool use to prevent chlorine damage. **Q: How long do budget fitness tracker bands typically last?** A: Standard silicone bands last 12-18 months with daily use. Most manufacturers sell replacement bands for $10-15, making band wear a minor long-term cost consideration. **Q: Do I need a smartphone to use these fitness trackers?** A: While basic functionality works independently, initial setup and detailed analytics require smartphone connectivity. Most features depend on the companion app for full functionality. **Q: Can budget fitness trackers monitor blood pressure?** A: None of our recommended sub-$75 models include blood pressure monitoring. This feature remains limited to higher-end devices due to regulatory requirements and sensor costs. **Q: How often should I charge my fitness tracker?** A: Charging frequency depends on your chosen model and usage patterns. Plan for weekly charging with feature-rich devices, or bi-weekly charging with efficiency-focused options like the Mi Band 8. **Q: Are fitness tracker heart rate measurements reliable for medical purposes?** A: While accurate for fitness monitoring, these devices aren't medical-grade instruments.

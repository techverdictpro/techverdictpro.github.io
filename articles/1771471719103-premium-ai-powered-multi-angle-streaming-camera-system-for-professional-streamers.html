Minimum recommended specs include an 8-core processor and 16GB RAM, though 32GB provides better performance margins for simultaneous streaming and recording. **Can these systems work with existing streaming software?** Most multi-angle systems integrate with OBS Studio, Streamlabs, and XSplit through standard video input protocols. AI systems typically output a single processed feed, while manual systems may require software-based switching setup. **How much bandwidth do multi-angle streams require?** Bandwidth requirements depend on output resolution and quality settings rather than input camera count. A single 1080p60 stream uses the same bandwidth regardless of how many cameras generate the content. **Do AI cameras work in low-light conditions?** AI processing relies on visual recognition, which degrades in poor lighting. Most systems include IR illuminators or enhanced low-light sensors, but optimal performance requires adequate ambient lighting. **Can I mix different camera brands in one system?** Generally no. Multi-angle systems require consistent color profiles and timing synchronization best achieved with matched camera sets. Mixed systems often produce noticeable quality differences between angles. **What happens if the AI makes wrong switching decisions?** Most systems include manual override capabilities, allowing real-time correction of AI decisions. Learning algorithms also improve over time based on user feedback and usage patterns. **Are these systems suitable for outdoor streaming?** Weather resistance varies significantly between models.

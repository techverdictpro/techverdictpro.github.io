## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right Budget 4K Capture Card Understanding your specific use case is crucial when selecting a budget capture card. Different streaming scenarios demand different feature priorities. **For Console Gaming**: Prioritize reliable 4K HDR passthrough over recording resolution. Console outputs rarely exceed 1080p60 for most games, making 4K recording capabilities less critical than seamless passthrough. The Elgato HD60 X excels here with its zero-lag technology. **For PC Gaming**: Consider your graphics card compatibility carefully. NVIDIA and AMD cards sometimes behave differently with specific capture devices. USB 3.0 bandwidth also becomes crucial—ensure your PC has dedicated USB controllers rather than shared hubs. **For Mobile Content Creation**: Portability and driver-free operation take precedence. The AVerMedia Live Gamer Mini's compact size and plug-and-play functionality make it ideal for creators working across multiple locations. **Software Ecosystem Considerations**: Don't underestimate the importance of good software. Elgato's Game Capture software significantly reduces the learning curve for beginners, while experienced users might prefer direct OBS integration regardless of the included software quality. **Future-Proofing**: While true 4K recording remains expensive, ensure your chosen device supports 4K passthrough for when you eventually upgrade your recording setup. This prevents the need for immediate hardware replacement as your content creation needs evolve. Budget allocation should prioritize reliable basic functionality over flashy features. A $70 device that consistently works beats a $90 device with premium features that work sporadically.

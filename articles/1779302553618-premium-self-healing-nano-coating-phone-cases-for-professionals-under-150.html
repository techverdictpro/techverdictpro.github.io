Frequently Asked Questions

How does self-healing technology actually work?

Self-healing phone cases use specialized polymers that return to their original shape when scratched. The molecules reconnect automatically at room temperature, eliminating minor surface damage within hours or days.

Do self-healing cases work with screen protectors?

Yes, self-healing cases work perfectly with screen protectors and tempered glass. The case protects the back and sides while your screen protector handles the front surface independently.

How long do self-healing properties last?

Most quality cases maintain self-healing effectiveness for 12-18 months with normal use. The nano-coating gradually wears down over time but continues providing basic scratch resistance even after healing properties diminish.

Can deep scratches or cuts self-heal?

Self-healing technology only works on surface-level scratches and micro-abrasions. Deep cuts, punctures, or gouges that penetrate the coating layer cannot repair themselves automatically.

Do self-healing cases affect wireless charging?

Premium self-healing cases support wireless charging at full speeds. The nano-coating layers are thin enough not to interfere with electromagnetic fields used in wireless charging systems.

Are self-healing cases worth the extra cost?

For professionals who want their phones to look pristine, the technology delivers genuine value. Cases that actually work cost $65-130 but eliminate the need for frequent case replacements due to scratching.

How can I test if the self-healing is working?

Lightly scratch the case with a coin or key, then wait 24-48 hours at room temperature. Genuine self-healing cases will show noticeable improvement or complete scratch elimination in that timeframe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do anti-radiation phone cases actually work?

Yes, quality cases with proper EMF shielding materials can block 99%+ of electromagnetic radiation. However, effectiveness varies significantly between manufacturers, making independent lab testing results crucial for verification.

Will these cases interfere with my phone's signal strength?

Properly designed anti-radiation cases should not interfere with signal quality. They shield radiation directed toward your body while allowing normal communication with cell towers and WiFi networks.

How do I know if a case is really protecting me?

Look for cases with independent lab testing from FCC-accredited facilities. Avoid products making vague claims without specific test results or blocking percentages across different frequency ranges.

Can I still use wireless charging with these cases?

Most anti-radiation cases are not compatible with wireless charging due to the metal shielding materials. You'll need to remove the phone from the case or choose models specifically designed for wireless charging compatibility.

Do these cases protect against 5G radiation?

Quality anti-radiation cases protect against 5G frequencies along with older 4G, 3G, WiFi, and Bluetooth radiation. Verify that any case you consider has been tested specifically for 5G frequency ranges.

How long do anti-radiation cases maintain their effectiveness?

Well-made cases maintain protection for several years with normal use. The shielding materials don't degrade significantly over time, though physical damage to the case can compromise effectiveness.

Are expensive cases always better than cheaper options?

Not necessarily. While build quality often improves with price, radiation protection effectiveness plateaus around the $70-$90 price range. More expensive cases typically offer better materials and aesthetics rather than superior protection.

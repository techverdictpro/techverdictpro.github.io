Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between IPS and other panel types?

IPS panels offer superior color accuracy and viewing angles compared to TN panels, while providing faster response times than VA panels. For beginners, IPS represents the best balance of image quality and performance.

Do I need more than 60Hz for office work?

Higher refresh rates like 75Hz or 144Hz provide smoother scrolling and reduced eye strain even in office applications. The difference is subtle but noticeable during extended use.

Can these monitors handle gaming?

All our recommendations handle casual gaming well. The AOC 24G2 excels at competitive gaming, while others work fine for single-player games and less demanding titles.

Is 1080p resolution enough for 24-inch screens?

Yes, 1080p provides excellent sharpness at 24 inches with a pixel density of 92 PPI. Text appears crisp and images look detailed without requiring expensive graphics cards to drive higher resolutions.

What cables do I need?

Most modern devices use HDMI connections, included with all our recommendations. Older computers may require VGA or DVI adapters. Check your device's output ports before purchasing.

How important is color accuracy for beginners?

Very important for any visual work including photo editing, design, or content creation. Even casual users benefit from accurate colors that make photos and videos appear as intended.

Should I calibrate my monitor?

Basic calibration improves any monitor's performance. Windows and macOS include built-in calibration tools that provide significant improvements without additional hardware purchases.

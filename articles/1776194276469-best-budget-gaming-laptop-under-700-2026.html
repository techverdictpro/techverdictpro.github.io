Best Budget Gaming Laptops Under $700 in 2026: Top Picks

Introduction

Budget gaming laptops have come remarkably far in 2026, delivering playable frame rates on popular titles without breaking the bank. The under-$700 segment now offers genuine 1080p gaming capability, a significant leap from the compromised experiences of just a few years ago.

The market has been driven by AMD's latest APUs and Intel's integrated graphics improvements, alongside manufacturers getting more aggressive with pricing. These machines won't run Cyberpunk 2077 at ultra settings, but they'll handle Fortnite, Valorant, and even some AAA titles at medium settings quite well.

This review focuses on laptops that balance gaming performance with everyday usability. We tested each machine with popular games, evaluated build quality, and assessed real-world battery life during our month-long testing period.

Whether you're a student on a tight budget or a casual gamer looking for an entry point, these laptops prove you don't need to spend $1,200+ to enjoy PC gaming on the go.

How to Choose the Right Gaming Laptop

Your first consideration should be which games you actually want to play. Esports titles like Valorant, CS2, and League of Legends will run well on any laptop in this guide. More demanding single-player games require careful settings management but remain playable.

Display quality matters more than many buyers realize. A 144Hz refresh rate helps in competitive games, while better color accuracy improves the overall visual experience. Don't get caught up in refresh rate numbers if the panel quality is poor.

Consider your usage beyond gaming. Students need good keyboards and professional-looking designs. Content creators should prioritize CPU performance and display color accuracy. Pure gamers can focus on GPU power and cooling performance.

Build quality varies significantly in this price range. Handle laptops in person when possible, paying attention to keyboard feel and screen hinge sturdiness. These machines need to survive years of use to justify their purchase.

Battery life expectations should remain realistic. Plan for 4-6 hours of productivity work and 1-2 hours of gaming. Gaming laptops are essentially portable desktops rather than true all-day mobile computers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can these laptops run AAA games?

Yes, but with compromises. Most recent AAA titles will run at 1080p medium settings with frame rates between 30-45fps. Older AAA games from 2020-2022 often achieve 50+ fps at medium-high settings.

How important is RAM upgradability?

Very important. Most budget laptops ship with 8GB, which limits performance in modern games. Look for models with accessible RAM slots, and plan to upgrade to 16GB within the first year if possible.

Should I buy now or wait for better deals?

Laptop prices in this segment remain relatively stable throughout the year. Back-to-school sales in July-August and Black Friday offer the best discounts, typically 10-15% off regular prices.

How long will these laptops stay relevant for gaming?

Expect 2-3 years of solid performance in current games, with settings gradually dropping from medium to low. Esports titles will remain playable much longer, often 4-5+ years with good performance.

Is the display upgrade worth it?

Higher refresh rate displays (120Hz+) make a noticeable difference in competitive games. However, color accuracy improvements often matter more for single-player games and general usage than refresh rate alone.

Can I connect an external GPU later?

Most budget laptops lack Thunderbolt 4 support needed for external GPUs. The few that do support eGPUs require significant additional investment that often exceeds buying a better laptop initially.

How much should I spend on accessories?

Budget for a gaming mouse ($30-50) and possibly external headphones ($50-100). A laptop cooling pad can help with thermal performance but isn't essential for most users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does sapphire crystal screen protection affect Face ID functionality?

No, properly installed sapphire crystal protectors maintain full Face ID compatibility. The infrared sensors penetrate the crystal without interference, preserving security features and unlock speed.

How do I remove air bubbles from tempered glass protectors?

Use a credit card wrapped in microfiber cloth to gently push bubbles toward edges. Most bubbles resolve within 24-48 hours as adhesive settles. Persistent bubbles may indicate dust particles requiring reinstallation.

Can screen protectors survive multiple drops?

Quality protectors typically survive several moderate impacts before requiring replacement. Sapphire crystal offers superior longevity compared to tempered glass alternatives, though severe impacts may cause cracking regardless of material.

Do screen protectors interfere with wireless charging?

Modern screen protectors don't affect wireless charging efficiency. The 0.33mm maximum thickness remains well within Qi charging tolerances, maintaining full charging speed and reliability.

Which screen protector material offers the best value?

Tempered glass provides optimal value for most users, balancing protection, clarity, and cost. Sapphire crystal justifies premium pricing for users requiring maximum durability and longevity.

How often should I replace my screen protector?

Replace protectors when cracks appear, touch sensitivity degrades, or optical clarity diminishes. Sapphire crystal typically lasts 12-18 months, while tempered glass requires replacement every 6-12 months depending on usage.

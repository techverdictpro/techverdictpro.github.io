How to Choose the Right Soundbar

Room size matters most when selecting a budget soundbar. Small rooms under 150 square feet can work well with compact models, while larger spaces benefit from soundbars with dedicated subwoofers or higher power ratings.

Consider your primary content type. If you mainly watch dialogue-heavy shows and movies, prioritize soundbars with clear voice technology or dedicated center channels. Music listeners should focus on models with balanced frequency response and multiple EQ options.

Connectivity needs vary by setup. HDMI ARC simplifies connections and allows TV remote control, while optical connections work with older TVs. Bluetooth adds wireless streaming from phones and tablets, which many users find essential.

Budget allocation should prioritize audio quality over features at this price point. A $80 soundbar that sounds great beats a $100 model with mediocre audio and extra connectivity options you won't use regularly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a separate subwoofer with these soundbars?

Most budget soundbars include built-in subwoofers that provide adequate bass for small rooms. Separate subwoofers improve low-end response but add complexity and cost that may not be justified under $100.

Will these work with older TVs?

Yes, most budget soundbars include optical and aux inputs that work with older televisions. HDMI connectivity is preferred but not required for basic functionality.

How much power do I need for a small room?

Power ratings can be misleading, but 100-160 watts total power typically provides sufficient volume for rooms under 200 square feet. Focus on audio quality over raw wattage numbers.

Can I use these soundbars with my computer?

Absolutely. Bluetooth connectivity and aux inputs make these soundbars excellent computer speakers, often providing better audio than dedicated PC speakers in the same price range.

Do budget soundbars support surround sound?

True surround sound requires multiple speakers, which isn't possible under $100. However, many budget soundbars include virtual surround processing that creates a wider soundstage than TV speakers.

How important is HDMI ARC connectivity?

HDMI ARC simplifies setup and allows TV remote volume control, but it's not essential. Optical connections provide identical audio quality, though you'll need to use the soundbar's remote for volume adjustments.

Should I wait for sales before buying?

Budget soundbars frequently go on sale during major shopping events. However, the price differences are usually $10-20, which may not be worth waiting months if you need immediate audio improvement.

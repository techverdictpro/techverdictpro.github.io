Buying Guide: Choosing Your Ideal Tablet Stand

Selecting the right tablet stand requires matching features to your specific usage patterns. Consider these key factors before making your decision:

Stability Requirements

Desktop users should prioritize weight and base width. Stands exceeding one pound with bases over 6 inches provide superior stability for typing, video calls, and extended viewing. Mobile users can sacrifice some stability for portability, but ensure the stand handles your tablet's weight plus any case.

Adjustment Range

Infinite adjustment offers maximum flexibility but may sacrifice holding power. Preset positions provide reliable locking but limit customization. Consider your primary uses: video calls benefit from precise angle control, while media consumption works fine with preset options.

Device Compatibility

Verify both size and thickness limits. Cases, keyboards, and screen protectors add bulk that may exceed stated specifications. Future device compatibility matters too—stands supporting up to 13 inches accommodate potential upgrades.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will these stands work with tablets in cases?

Most stands accommodate cases up to 10-15mm thick. Keyboard cases and rugged protection may exceed these limits. Check thickness specifications before purchasing.

Can I use a tablet stand for video calls without wobbling?

Heavier stands with wider bases minimize wobble during gesturing. The Lamicall and OMOTON T1 perform best for video conferencing applications.

Do aluminum stands scratch tablets?

Quality stands include silicone or rubber padding at contact points. Avoid stands without protective surfaces, regardless of price.

What's the difference between fixed and adjustable stands?

Fixed stands offer specific angles (usually 2-3 options) but cost less and often provide better stability. Adjustable stands offer infinite positioning but may develop looseness over time.

Can these stands support laptops?

While some stands handle small laptops, they're optimized for tablet weight and dimensions. Dedicated laptop stands provide better ergonomics and cooling.

How do I maintain my tablet stand?

Aluminum stands require minimal maintenance—occasional cleaning with a damp cloth suffices. Avoid harsh chemicals that might damage protective coatings or silicone components.

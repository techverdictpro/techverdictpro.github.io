Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are budget fitness trackers for step counting?

Modern budget trackers achieve 95-98% accuracy for step counting during normal walking. Accuracy drops slightly during activities like pushing shopping carts or carrying items, but remains acceptable for general fitness monitoring.

Do I need GPS in my first fitness tracker?

Built-in GPS helps track distance and pace during outdoor activities without carrying your phone. It's useful but not essential for beginners focused on daily activity monitoring rather than serious training.

How long do fitness tracker batteries typically last?

Battery life varies from 5-18 days depending on the model and usage patterns. Always-on displays, GPS usage, and frequent notifications reduce battery life significantly. Most users charge weekly regardless of rated capacity.

Can fitness trackers monitor sleep accurately?

Sleep tracking accuracy has improved dramatically in recent years. Most devices correctly identify sleep and wake times within 10-15 minutes and provide useful insights into sleep patterns and quality.

Are fitness trackers waterproof?

Most modern trackers offer water resistance suitable for swimming and showering. Look for ratings of 5ATM or 50 meters. Avoid saltwater exposure and rinse devices after swimming in chlorinated pools.

What's the difference between fitness trackers and smartwatches?

Fitness trackers focus primarily on health and activity monitoring with longer battery life. Smartwatches offer broader functionality including apps, calls, and messaging but typically require daily charging.

How do I clean and maintain my fitness tracker?

Clean trackers weekly with mild soap and water, avoiding harsh chemicals. Remove the band regularly to prevent skin irritation and bacterial buildup. Replace bands every 6-12 months depending on usage intensity.

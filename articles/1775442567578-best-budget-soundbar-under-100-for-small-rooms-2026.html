How to Choose the Right Soundbar

Room size should drive your decision more than raw specifications. Soundbars under $100 work best in spaces under 200 square feet where moderate volume levels suffice. Larger rooms benefit from more powerful (and expensive) options.

Consider your TV's age and available connections. Older sets may lack optical outputs, requiring soundbars with 3.5mm or RCA inputs. Newer TVs with HDMI ARC provide the most flexibility and often support advanced audio features.

Think about your primary content types. Dialogue-heavy shows benefit from soundbars emphasizing midrange clarity, while action movies require better bass response. Music listening demands more balanced frequency response across all ranges.

Budget allocation matters significantly in this price range. Spending the full $100 typically provides noticeably better build quality and sound than $40-50 options. However, diminishing returns mean $150+ models offer minimal improvements for small room use.

Installation requirements vary considerably between models. Wall-mounting capability, cable management, and remote control quality can significantly impact daily satisfaction with your purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do budget soundbars really improve TV audio?

Yes, even basic soundbars provide clearer dialogue and better stereo separation than built-in TV speakers. The improvement is most noticeable with speech-heavy content like news and dramas.

Can I add a separate subwoofer later?

Some budget soundbars include subwoofer outputs for future expansion, but most are sealed systems. Check specifications carefully if bass expansion is important to your future plans.

How important is Bluetooth connectivity?

Bluetooth allows music streaming from phones and tablets, significantly expanding the soundbar's utility beyond TV audio. Models with aptX codec support provide better wireless audio quality.

Will a soundbar work with my older TV?

Most budget soundbars include multiple connection options. TVs from the last 15 years typically have at least one compatible input, though you may need additional cables.

How much space do I need above my TV?

Wall-mounted soundbars require 2-3 inches clearance above the unit. Measure carefully, as some models are taller than others and may block TV sensors or ports.

Is professional installation necessary?

Budget soundbars are designed for DIY installation. Most connect with a single cable and include mounting hardware. Professional installation is rarely worth the cost for these simple systems.

How long do budget soundbars typically last?

Quality budget soundbars should provide 3-5 years of reliable service with normal use. Avoid ultra-cheap models from unknown brands, as build quality and longevity suffer significantly.

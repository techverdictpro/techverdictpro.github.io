Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is GPS tracking under $300?

Modern fitness trackers achieve 3-5 meter accuracy in open areas, with premium models like the Garmin Vivosmart 7 matching dedicated GPS devices. Urban environments and tree coverage can reduce precision to 8-10 meters.

Can fitness trackers replace dedicated sleep studies?

While consumer trackers provide valuable insights, they cannot diagnose sleep disorders or replace medical sleep studies. They excel at identifying patterns and trends in your personal sleep quality over time.

How long do fitness tracker batteries actually last?

Real-world battery life typically falls 10-20% short of manufacturer claims. Heavy GPS usage, frequent notifications, and always-on displays significantly impact longevity. Most users charge weekly regardless of stated battery life.

Are fitness trackers waterproof for swimming?

Devices rated 5ATM or 50 meters handle swimming and water sports safely. Avoid hot showers or saunas, which can damage seals through rapid temperature changes and steam exposure.

Do I need smartphone connectivity for GPS tracking?

All recommended trackers include independent GPS chips that function without phone connectivity. However, smartphone pairing enables faster satellite acquisition and detailed map displays during activities.

How accurate is sleep stage tracking?

Consumer sleep trackers achieve 70-80% accuracy for basic sleep/wake detection but struggle with precise sleep stage identification. Focus on trends and patterns rather than individual night measurements.

Can fitness trackers cause skin irritation?

Prolonged wear, especially during sweaty workouts, can cause irritation in sensitive users. Choose breathable bands, clean regularly, and alternate wrist positions to minimize skin contact issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can these drives handle continuous 8K video recording?
A: The Samsung T9 and SanDisk PRO-G40 can sustain the write speeds required for most 8K codecs. However, always test with your specific camera and codec combination before critical shoots.

Q: Do I need Thunderbolt 3 for video editing?
A: Not necessarily. Modern USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 provides sufficient bandwidth for most editing workflows. Thunderbolt 3 offers advantages for Mac users and when working with multiple high-resolution streams simultaneously.

Q: How important is sustained vs. peak performance?
A: Extremely important. Peak speeds matter for small file transfers, but sustained performance determines how quickly you can backup projects or export finished videos. Always prioritize sustained write speeds for content creation.

Q: Are these drives compatible with cameras for direct recording?
A: Most modern cameras support USB-C external recording, but compatibility varies by manufacturer and model. Always verify compatibility before relying on external recording for critical projects.

Q: What's the expected lifespan of an 8TB portable SSD?
A: Quality drives from reputable manufacturers typically last 5-10 years with normal usage. Monitor drive health using manufacturer software and maintain regular backups regardless of drive age.

Q: Do these drives work with iPads and Android devices?
A: Yes, all recommended drives support mobile devices with USB-C ports. However, power consumption may drain device batteries faster, and some devices may require powered hubs for reliable operation.

Q: Is hardware encryption worth the performance penalty?
A: For professional content creators handling sensitive material, hardware encryption provides essential security with minimal performance impact on modern drives. The Samsung T9 and SanDisk PRO-G40 implement encryption efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do DIY phone cases typically last?

Well-made DIY cases can last 6-12 months with daily use. Quality depends heavily on materials used and application technique. Premium kits like CraftCase Pro typically outlast budget alternatives.

Can I remove and redo designs if I make mistakes?

Most vinyl and sticker-based designs can be carefully removed and reapplied within the first few hours. Once fully adhered, removal may damage the underlying case surface.

Do DIY cases provide adequate drop protection?

Quality DIY kits offer protection comparable to basic commercial cases. However, they typically can't match specialized rugged cases designed for extreme conditions or heavy-duty use.

What phones are compatible with these kits?

Most kits support iPhone models from the 12 series onward and Samsung Galaxy S20+ models. Always verify compatibility before purchasing, as newer phone models may not be included.

Can I use my own photos and designs?

Yes, most kits accept custom designs through templates or direct printing. Photo resolution should be at least 300 DPI for best results, and designs should account for camera cutouts and button placement.

How difficult is the application process?

Modern kits are designed for beginners, with most projects taking 15-30 minutes to complete. Following included instructions carefully is essential for professional-looking results.

Are the materials safe for daily handling?

All tested kits use non-toxic materials safe for daily contact. However, some specialty finishes may cause skin irritation in sensitive individuals – test on a small area first if concerned.

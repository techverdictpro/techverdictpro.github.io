How to Choose the Right Budget Soundbar

Room size should drive your primary decision. Soundbars under 20 inches work best in spaces smaller than 150 square feet, while rooms up to 250 square feet benefit from 24-30 inch models. Larger soundbars in small rooms often create overwhelming bass that muddies dialogue.

Connection options matter more than fancy features in this price range. Prioritize HDMI ARC compatibility for newer TVs, with optical input as backup. Bluetooth adds versatility for music streaming but shouldn't be the primary selling point for TV audio improvement.

Power ratings can be misleading at budget prices. Focus on reviews mentioning dialogue clarity and distortion levels rather than peak wattage numbers. A 60-watt soundbar with clean audio outperforms a 100-watt model that distorts at higher volumes.

Consider your primary use case when evaluating features. Gaming modes and multiple EQ presets add value for enthusiasts but represent wasted cost for casual TV viewing. Simple, well-executed audio processing often trumps feature-heavy options at these price points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do budget soundbars really improve TV audio?

Yes, even basic soundbars provide noticeable improvement over TV speakers. Modern TVs prioritize thin designs that leave no room for quality speakers, making any external audio solution a significant upgrade.

How much space do I need around a soundbar?

Most soundbars need 2-3 inches of clearance on each side for proper airflow. Enclosed entertainment centers may trap heat and cause overheating during extended use.

Can I use a budget soundbar for music streaming?

Budget soundbars work adequately for casual music listening via Bluetooth but lack the frequency response for serious music enjoyment. They're optimized for dialogue and basic entertainment audio.

Will my neighbors hear the bass from these soundbars?

Budget soundbars under $100 rarely produce enough bass to cause neighbor complaints. Most prioritize dialogue clarity over deep bass response that could disturb adjacent apartments.

How long do budget soundbars typically last?

Quality budget soundbars should provide 3-5 years of regular use. Avoid models with unusually light weight or hollow construction that suggest poor internal component quality.

Do I need a separate subwoofer with these soundbars?

Most budget soundbars include basic bass processing that's adequate for small rooms. Separate subwoofers often cost more than these entire soundbar systems and may overwhelm small spaces.

Can I wall mount budget soundbars?

Most budget soundbars include wall mounting hardware or support standard VESA mounting patterns. Check weight limits and ensure proper wall anchoring for safety.

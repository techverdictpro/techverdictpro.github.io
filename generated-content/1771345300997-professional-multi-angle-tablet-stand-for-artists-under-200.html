## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right Stand for Your Needs ### Essential Features to Consider **Stability and Weight**: Look for stands weighing at least 2 pounds. Lighter options may save space but often shift during use, disrupting creative flow. The base design matters as much as weight—wider footprints and rubberized bottoms provide superior grip. **Adjustment Range**: Consider your actual working positions rather than maximum specifications. Most artists work within a 30-60 degree range, making smooth adjustment within this zone more valuable than extreme angles. Test adjustment mechanisms when possible—sticky or stiff movement becomes frustrating over time. **Material Quality**: Aluminum offers the best balance of durability, weight, and aesthetics for most users. Plastic stands can work for light use but may not withstand professional demands. Steel provides excellent stability but adds bulk. Wood stands offer unique aesthetics but require more maintenance. **Device Compatibility**: Verify compatibility with your specific tablet size and any cases or accessories you use regularly. Some stands accommodate bare tablets only, while others work with keyboard cases or thick protective covers. ### Budget Recommendations by User Type **Hobbyist Artists ($30-$60)**: The Nulaxy A4 provides adequate functionality for occasional use. Verify compatibility with your specific device dimensions, including any cases or accessories.

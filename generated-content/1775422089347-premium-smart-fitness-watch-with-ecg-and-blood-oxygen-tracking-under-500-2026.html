Frequently Asked Questions

Are ECG readings actually useful for average users?

ECG monitoring can detect irregular heart rhythms like atrial fibrillation, but it's not a replacement for regular medical checkups. Most users find it reassuring rather than medically essential.

How accurate is blood oxygen monitoring on fitness watches?

Consumer-grade pulse oximeters typically show 2-3% variance from medical devices. They're useful for trend tracking but shouldn't be relied upon for medical decisions.

Do I need to pay for premium subscriptions?

Garmin and Apple provide comprehensive features without subscriptions. Fitbit requires Premium for detailed insights, while Samsung offers basic and premium tiers.

Which watch has the best battery life?

Garmin leads with 10-14 days typical usage. Samsung and Fitbit last 2-3 days, while Apple requires daily charging with moderate use.

Can these watches detect heart attacks?

No consumer fitness watch can detect heart attacks. They can identify irregular rhythms that warrant medical attention, but they're not diagnostic devices.

Are these watches accurate enough for serious training?

Yes, our top picks achieved 95%+ accuracy compared to chest strap monitors. They're suitable for all but the most demanding professional athletic applications.

How important is water resistance?

All our recommended models offer 5ATM rating, suitable for swimming and showering. This level of protection is essential for 24/7 wear and exercise tracking.

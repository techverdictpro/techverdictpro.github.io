## Frequently Asked Questions **Q: Is wireless latency noticeable in competitive FPS gaming?** A: Modern wireless gaming mice from established manufacturers operate with 1ms latency, identical to wired connections. Professional esports players regularly compete with wireless mice at the highest levels. **Q: How important is DPI for FPS gaming?** A: Most professional FPS players utilize 400-1600 DPI combined with low in-game sensitivity. Modern sensors handle these ranges flawlessly, making maximum DPI specifications largely irrelevant for practical use. **Q: Do ultralight mice sacrifice durability?** A: Quality ultralight mice maintain excellent durability through advanced materials and engineering. Avoid extremely cheap lightweight options that may compromise structural integrity. **Q: Should I prioritize ergonomics or performance features?** A: Comfort enables sustained performance during extended gaming sessions. An ergonomically suitable mouse with adequate performance typically outperforms an uncomfortable mouse with superior specifications. **Q: How often should I replace my gaming mouse?** A: Quality gaming mice typically last 2-3 years of intensive use. Replace when switches develop inconsistency, sensors accumulate permanent debris, or physical wear affects grip comfort. **Q: Are expensive mousepads necessary with premium mice?** A: Quality mousepads optimize sensor performance and provide consistent glide characteristics. A $25-40 mousepad represents excellent value when paired with premium mice. **Q: Do gaming mice work effectively on different surfaces?** A: Modern optical sensors perform excellently on appropriate mouse surfaces but may struggle on glass, highly reflective, or transparent materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is titanium really worth the premium price?

Yes, for professionals who depend on their phones daily. Titanium offers superior impact resistance, scratch resistance, and longevity compared to plastic or aluminum cases. The material doesn't degrade over time like rubber compounds.

Do titanium cases interfere with wireless charging?

Quality titanium cases support wireless charging without issues. Avoid cases with thick back plates or magnetic components near the charging coil. All our tested cases worked with standard Qi chargers.

How much protection do I actually need?

Most phone damage occurs from drops under 6 feet onto hard surfaces. Cases rated for 10+ foot drops provide safety margin for unexpected impacts. Consider your clumsiness factor and work environment risks.

Will titanium cases scratch my phone?

Premium cases use soft interior linings to prevent scratching. Avoid cheap titanium cases with rough internal surfaces. Dust and debris cause more scratching than the case material itself.

Do these cases work with screen protectors?

Yes, but fit tolerances become critical. Cases with raised bezels provide better screen protection than relying solely on protectors. Test fit your specific screen protector before purchasing.

How long do titanium cases last?

Quality titanium cases can outlast multiple phone upgrades. The material doesn't yellow, crack, or lose elasticity like plastics. Many manufacturers offer trade-in programs for new phone models.

Are there waterproof titanium cases available?

True waterproofing requires sealed construction that most titanium cases don't provide. Look for IP68-rated cases if water protection is essential, though this limits design options and adds bulk significantly.

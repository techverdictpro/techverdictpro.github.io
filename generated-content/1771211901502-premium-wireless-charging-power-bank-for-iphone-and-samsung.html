Usage Tips and Best Practices

To maximize your experience with the Premium Wireless Charging Power Bank, consider these expert tips:

Optimal Placement: Center your device on the wireless charging pad and ensure the charging icon appears on your phone's screen. Misalignment can result in slower charging or interrupted sessions.

Temperature Management: Avoid wireless charging in direct sunlight or extremely hot environments. If the power bank feels warm during use, this is normal, but excessive heat may indicate a problem.

Battery Maintenance: Recharge the power bank when it reaches 20-30% capacity for optimal battery longevity. Avoid completely draining the battery regularly.

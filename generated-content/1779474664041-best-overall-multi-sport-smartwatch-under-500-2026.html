How to Choose the Right Multi-Sport Smartwatch

Your primary sport should guide your decision more than any other factor. Runners benefit most from advanced GPS accuracy and training load metrics, while swimmers need reliable water resistance and stroke detection. Cyclists should prioritize battery life and compatibility with external sensors like power meters and cadence sensors.

Budget considerations extend beyond the initial purchase price. Garmin and Polar offer premium subscription services (Connect IQ and Polar Flow Premium) that unlock advanced features, while Amazfit includes most functionality without additional fees. Factor in potential accessory costs like chest straps for more accurate heart rate monitoring during intense workouts.

Battery life requirements vary dramatically based on your activities. Weekend warriors can manage with 15-hour GPS life, but ultrarunners and backpackers need 24+ hours minimum. Consider whether you can realistically charge every few days or need a watch that goes a full week between charges.

Smart features range from essential notifications to full smartphone replacement capabilities. Determine whether you need music storage, contactless payments, and app downloads, or if basic call and text notifications suffice for your lifestyle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which smartwatch has the most accurate GPS for running?

The Garmin Forerunner 265 and Coros Pace 3 both offer dual-band GPS that provides exceptional accuracy, typically within 1-2% of actual distance. Both outperformed single-frequency competitors in our testing across various environments.

Can these watches track swimming in pools and open water?

All our recommended models handle pool and open water swimming, with automatic stroke detection and lap counting. The Garmin Forerunner 265 offers the most comprehensive swimming metrics, including SWOLF scoring and stroke efficiency analysis.

How accurate is optical heart rate monitoring during high-intensity workouts?

Optical sensors struggle with rapid heart rate changes during interval training. The Polar Pacer Pro performs best in this category, but serious athletes should consider pairing any watch with a chest strap for maximum accuracy during intense sessions.

Do these watches work with third-party fitness apps like Strava?

Yes, all models sync with popular platforms including Strava, Training Peaks, and MyFitnessPal. Garmin offers the broadest app ecosystem, while Amazfit has the most limited third-party integration.

What's the real-world battery life during GPS activities?

Expect 15-24 hours depending on the model and GPS settings. The Coros Pace 3 leads at 24 hours, while the Garmin Forerunner 265 provides about 20 hours. Using power-saving modes can extend this significantly.

Are these watches suitable for strength training?

All models include strength training modes with rep counting and rest timers. The Garmin Forerunner 265 offers the most detailed strength metrics, including muscle group tracking and recovery time estimation.

Can I make phone calls or play music from these watches?

The Garmin Forerunner 265 supports music storage and Bluetooth headphone connectivity but not phone calls. Other models in our lineup focus on fitness features rather than communication capabilities.

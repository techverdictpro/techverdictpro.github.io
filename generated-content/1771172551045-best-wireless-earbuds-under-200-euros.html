Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are wireless earbuds under €200 worth buying over cheaper alternatives?

Absolutely. Earbuds in this price range offer significantly better sound quality, build quality, and features compared to budget options. You get advanced ANC, premium codecs, longer battery life, and superior comfort.

2. How important is active noise cancellation?

ANC is crucial if you frequently travel, commute on public transport, or work in noisy environments. However, if you primarily use earbuds at home or in quiet settings, passive isolation might suffice.

3. Which earbuds offer the best call quality?

The Jabra Elite 85t excels in call quality thanks to its MultiSensor Voice technology, while the Sony WF-1000XM4 also performs exceptionally well in various environments.

4. Can I use these earbuds for sports and workouts?

Yes, all recommended models offer at least IPX4 water resistance. The Cambridge Audio Melomania 1+ with IPX5 rating provides the best protection for intense workouts.

5. Do these earbuds work well with both Android and iOS devices?

All featured earbuds work with both platforms, though some features may be optimized for specific ecosystems. LDAC support works best with Android devices, while AAC is optimized for iOS.

6. How long do the batteries typically last in real-world usage?

Real-world battery life is usually 10-20% less than advertised, depending on volume levels, ANC usage, and codec selection. Expect 6-8 hours from most earbuds with moderate usage.

7. Are firmware updates important for wireless earbuds?

Yes, firmware updates can improve sound quality, fix bugs, and add new features. Sony, Jabra, and Sennheiser are particularly good at providing regular updates.

In real-world testing, the Bluetooth 5.1 connection maintained stable audio up to approximately 25-30 feet in open areas, slightly less in environments with obstacles or interference. This meets most practical usage requirements.

6. How durable is the carabiner clip mechanism?

The integrated clip is made from the same durable plastic as the speaker body and showed no signs of wear during extensive testing. It securely holds the speaker's weight and withstands repeated attachment and detachment.

7. Is there any mobile app for the JBL Clip 4?

JBL doesn't provide a dedicated app for the Clip 4, as it's designed to be a simple, plug-and-play device. All controls are handled through the physical buttons on the speaker or your connected device.

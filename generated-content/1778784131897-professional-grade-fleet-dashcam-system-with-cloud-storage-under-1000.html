How to Choose the Right Fleet Dashcam System

Budget considerations extend beyond initial hardware costs. Monthly service fees typically range from $25-50 per vehicle, while installation costs vary from DIY options to professional installation at $200-300 per vehicle.

Consider your fleet's specific needs carefully. Local delivery fleets benefit from different features than long-haul operations. Urban fleets might prioritize incident recording, while highway fleets focus on driver coaching and fatigue monitoring.

Cellular connectivity requirements depend on your coverage areas and reporting needs. Real-time alerts require constant connectivity, while basic systems can upload footage when vehicles return to WiFi range.

Integration capabilities matter for larger fleets. Systems that work with existing fleet management, dispatch, or ELD platforms reduce administrative overhead and improve adoption rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does fleet dashcam installation cost?

Professional installation typically costs $200-300 per vehicle, while DIY installation takes 1-2 hours with basic tools. Most systems include detailed installation guides and phone support.

What's included in monthly service fees?

Monthly fees cover cloud storage, cellular connectivity, software updates, and customer support. Costs range from $25-50 per vehicle depending on features and fleet size.

Can drivers disable the cameras?

Driver-facing cameras can typically be disabled through fleet management settings, but road-facing cameras usually cannot be turned off by drivers to ensure continuous incident recording.

How long is video footage stored?

Standard plans include 30 days of continuous recording plus unlimited event-triggered clips. Extended storage options are available for additional monthly fees.

Do these systems work with existing fleet management software?

Most professional systems offer API integrations or direct compatibility with popular fleet management platforms like Fleetio, Azuga, and GPS Insight.

What happens if cellular coverage is poor?

Systems buffer footage locally and upload when connectivity improves. Most include 32-64GB of local storage for several days of recording without connectivity.

Are there contracts or cancellation fees?

Most providers require 12-24 month contracts with early termination fees. Some offer month-to-month plans at higher monthly rates.

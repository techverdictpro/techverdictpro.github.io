## Frequently Asked Questions **Q: Can professional mesh systems replace traditional enterprise access points?** A: Modern professional mesh systems match or exceed the performance of traditional enterprise access points in most small-to-medium business scenarios. They offer similar security features, management capabilities, and device capacity while simplifying installation and reducing upfront costs. However, very large enterprises or those with specialized requirements may still benefit from dedicated enterprise solutions. **Q: How many devices can these systems realistically handle?** A: Testing reveals that high-end systems like the ASUS ET12 and Netgear SXK80 comfortably manage 75-100+ concurrent devices without performance degradation. However, real-world capacity depends on usage patterns—offices with heavy video conferencing or large file transfers may experience limitations with fewer devices than those primarily using web-based applications. **Q: Do I need WiFi 6E for business use, or is WiFi 6 sufficient?** A: WiFi 6E provides significant advantages in congested office environments by utilizing the less crowded 6GHz spectrum. For offices in dense urban areas or buildings with many wireless networks, 6E systems deliver noticeably better performance and stability. Rural or less congested environments may not see dramatic improvements, making WiFi 6 systems more cost-effective. **Q: What's the difference between mesh backhaul and wired backhaul?** A: Wired backhaul connects mesh units via Ethernet cables, providing maximum performance and reliability. Wireless backhaul uses WiFi connections between units, offering easier installation but potentially reduced performance. Professional environments benefit from wired connections when possible, though modern systems with dedicated wireless backhaul bands perform well in most scenarios. **Q: Can I mix different mesh system brands or models?** A: Mesh systems require matching hardware from the same manufacturer to function properly. However, you can typically mix different models within a brand's ecosystem—such as using a high-end router with less expensive satellite units for cost optimization while maintaining compatibility. **Q: How often should professional mesh systems be upgraded?** A: Professional mesh systems typically provide 4-5 years of optimal performance before requiring replacement. However, rapidly evolving wireless standards and increasing device demands may necessitate earlier upgrades. Systems supporting firmware updates and expandable architectures offer longer useful lifespans than closed ecosystems. **Q: What happens if one mesh unit fails in a professional environment?** A: Quality mesh systems automatically route traffic around failed units, though coverage gaps may occur depending on your network topology. Most professional systems include warranty replacement programs and support redundant configurations to minimize downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are medical smartwatches FDA approved?

Many features on medical smartwatches have FDA clearance, particularly ECG monitoring and irregular heart rhythm detection. However, these devices are not replacements for professional medical equipment and should supplement, not replace, standard diagnostic tools.

Can medical smartwatches sync with hospital EMR systems?

Select models offer EMR integration with major systems like Epic, Cerner, and AllScripts. This feature typically requires institutional IT approval and proper security protocols to maintain HIPAA compliance.

How accurate are the health monitoring features?

Medical-grade smartwatches generally achieve 95-99% accuracy for heart rate and blood oxygen monitoring compared to clinical equipment. However, accuracy can vary based on skin tone, movement, and environmental conditions.

What's the typical battery life for medical smartwatches?

Battery life ranges from 18 hours (Apple Watch) to 14 days (Garmin Fenix), depending on features used. Most medical smartwatches offer 2-5 days of battery life with continuous health monitoring enabled.

Are these watches waterproof enough for medical environments?

All recommended medical smartwatches offer water resistance suitable for handwashing and sanitization. Most feature ratings of 5ATM or higher, providing protection against water immersion up to 50 meters.

Can medical smartwatches be sanitized safely?

Medical-grade smartwatches are designed to withstand repeated sanitization with alcohol-based solutions and UV sanitizing equipment. Check manufacturer guidelines for specific cleaning protocols.

Do medical smartwatches require special software or apps?

Most medical smartwatches work with standard companion apps, but professional features may require additional medical software subscriptions or institutional licenses for EMR integration and advanced clinical tools.

## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right 100W Power Bank ### Capacity Considerations Battery capacity directly impacts how many times you can charge your devices, but raw mAh numbers don't tell the complete story. Consider your primary laptop's battery size and daily usage patterns. A MacBook Pro 16-inch with a 100Wh battery will receive approximately 2.5 full charges from a 27,650mAh power bank accounting for conversion losses. For occasional use or day trips, 20,000mAh provides adequate emergency power. Daily commuters and frequent travelers should target 25,000-30,000mAh for all-day confidence. Remember that larger capacity means increased weight and size—find the balance that matches your carrying preferences. ### Power Output Requirements Not all 100W power banks deliver consistent performance across all ports simultaneously. Verify that your chosen model maintains full laptop charging speed while powering secondary devices. Some models throttle the primary port when additional ports are active, extending charging times significantly. Consider your multi-device charging needs. If you regularly charge a laptop, smartphone, and tablet simultaneously, prioritize models with smart power distribution that optimizes charging across all connected devices. ### Travel and Portability Factors Airline regulations limit power banks to 100Wh (approximately 27,000mAh), making this the maximum practical capacity for frequent flyers. TSA requires power banks in carry-on luggage, so factor size and weight into your travel gear allocation. Form factor impacts daily usability more than specifications suggest. Ultra-slim models slip easily into laptop bags but may sacrifice capacity or thermal performance. Chunky designs offer maximum capability at the cost of portability convenience.

## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right Monitor Arm ### Essential Compatibility Factors Before selecting any monitor arm, verify your display's VESA mounting pattern and weight. Most consumer monitors use either 75x75mm or 100x100mm VESA patterns, but larger displays may require 200x200mm or proprietary mounting solutions. Check your monitor's specifications or measure the mounting holes directly to ensure compatibility. Weight capacity should exceed your monitor's actual weight by at least 20% to ensure stable operation and account for the leverage forces created by the extended arm. Remember that ultrawide and curved monitors often weigh significantly more than their diagonal measurements might suggest. ### Desk Compatibility Considerations Desk thickness plays a crucial role in clamp-style installations. Measure your desk's thickness, including any cable management grommets or edge treatments that might interfere with mounting. Most arms accommodate desks from 0.4 to 3.9 inches thick, but verify compatibility before purchasing. Consider whether your desk surface can withstand clamping pressure without damage. Glass, thin wood veneer, and some composite materials may crack under the significant force required for secure mounting. In these cases, grommet-mount options or protective padding become essential. ### Adjustment Range Requirements Evaluate your specific positioning needs before prioritizing adjustment capabilities. Users with fixed seating positions may find basic tilt and height adjustment sufficient, while dynamic work environments benefit from comprehensive swivel and rotation options. Height adjustment becomes particularly important for sit-stand desk users or shared workstations. Ensure the arm's vertical travel range accommodates all intended users and seating configurations.

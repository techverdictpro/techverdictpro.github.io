Frequently Asked Questions

How many times can this power bank charge my phone?

The charging capacity depends on your device's battery size. For popular smartphones: iPhone 13 (approximately 2.2 charges), Samsung Galaxy S21 (about 2 charges), iPhone SE 2020 (roughly 3 charges), and Google Pixel 6 (approximately 1.8 charges). Remember that actual capacity is typically 10-15% lower than rated capacity due to conversion losses.

Is it safe to leave devices charging overnight?

Yes, the MultiProtect safety system includes overcharge protection that stops power delivery when devices reach full charge. However, as with any electronic device, it's generally good practice to avoid unnecessary extended charging sessions.

Can I use this power bank while it's being recharged?

While technically possible with some devices, simultaneous charging and discharging (pass-through charging) can generate additional heat and potentially reduce the power bank's lifespan. It's recommended to fully charge the power bank before use when possible. Check on Amazon →

How long does it take to recharge the power bank itself?

Using a standard 2A wall charger, the PowerCore 10000 takes approximately 4-5 hours to fully recharge from empty. The recharge time will be longer with lower-output chargers or when charging from a computer's USB port.

Will this work with my tablet?

Yes, the power bank works with most tablets, though charging speed and capacity will vary. iPad models typically charge at optimal speed, while larger tablets with bigger batteries may charge more slowly or require most of the power bank's capacity for a full charge.

What's included in the box?

How do I know when the power bank needs recharging?

The four-LED indicator system shows remaining capacity when you press the power button. When only one LED remains lit or starts blinking, it's time to recharge the power bank. Each LED represents approximately 25% capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these cases interfere with wireless charging?

All three featured cases support wireless charging, though charging may be slower than with thinner cases. The OtterBox and Pelican cases showed the most consistent wireless charging performance in our testing.

How do I clean these cases after exposure to construction dust and debris?

Remove your phone and rinse the case with water, using a soft brush for stubborn debris. The OtterBox's removable outer layer makes cleaning easiest, while the UAG requires more attention to its textured surfaces.

Will these cases fit in standard belt holsters or car mounts?

The added bulk means most aftermarket accessories won't fit. The OtterBox includes its own holster, while UAG and Pelican offer compatible mounting systems sold separately. Always check compatibility before purchasing accessories.

How often should I replace my rugged case?

With normal professional use, expect 1-2 years of protection. Replace immediately if you notice cracks, loose fitting, or compromised port covers. The Pelican's lifetime warranty eliminates this concern.

Are these cases waterproof?

None of these cases are fully waterproof, but all offer excellent water resistance for normal construction and outdoor conditions. For true waterproof protection, consider specialized submersible cases.

Do the cases affect phone reception or GPS accuracy?

Modern rugged cases use materials that don't interfere with cellular or GPS signals. Our testing showed no measurable difference in signal strength or GPS accuracy compared to uncased phones.

Can I use a screen protector with these cases?

Yes, tempered glass screen protectors work well with all three cases. In fact, we recommend using one for maximum screen protection, especially in construction environments with flying debris.

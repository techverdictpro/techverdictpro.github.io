Buying Guide: What to Consider Under $200

Weight Capacity vs. Your Needs

Budget electric desks typically support 130-176 pounds. Calculate your equipment weight: a 27" monitor weighs 15-20 pounds, laptops add 3-6 pounds, and accessories contribute another 10-15 pounds. Most users stay well under 50 pounds total, making any option sufficient.

Motor Configuration

Dual motors provide faster adjustment and better stability under heavy loads. Single-motor systems cost less but move slower and may struggle with maximum weight capacity. Choose dual motors if you frequently switch between sitting and standing.

Desktop Quality Considerations

Engineered wood dominates this price range. Look for scratch-resistant laminates and edge banding to prevent moisture damage. Solid wood costs more but offers superior durability and appearance.

Height Range Planning

Measure your ideal sitting and standing heights before purchasing. Users under 5'4" should verify minimum height compatibility, while those over 6'2" need maximum height confirmation. A 20-inch range typically accommodates most users.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do budget electric standing desks last?

Quality budget models last 3-5 years with regular use. Motor failures typically occur after 2-3 years of daily operation, though warranty coverage varies by manufacturer.

Are memory presets worth the extra cost?

Memory presets add $30-50 to desk prices. They're convenient but not essential—most users develop muscle memory for their preferred heights within a few weeks.

Can I upgrade a budget desk later?

Desktop replacement is possible, but motor or control system upgrades aren't cost-effective. Choose initial specifications carefully rather than planning upgrades.

How much assembly is required?

Budget desks require 30-60 minutes of assembly. Most include necessary tools, though a drill speeds up the process. Instructions vary in quality—check manufacturer reviews before purchasing.

What warranty should I expect?

Reputable brands offer 2-5 year warranties on frames and 1-2 years on electronics. Warranty service quality varies significantly—prioritize brands with established customer service.

Do budget desks work with monitor arms?

Most support standard C-clamp monitor arms, but desktop thickness and material affect compatibility. Verify specifications before purchasing monitor mounting accessories.

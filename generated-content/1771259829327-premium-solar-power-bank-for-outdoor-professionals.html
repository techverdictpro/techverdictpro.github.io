Frequently Asked Questions

How long does solar charging actually take?

In optimal conditions, solar charging can add 25% capacity in 6-8 hours. However, real-world conditions typically extend this to 10-12 hours for the same charge gain. Solar works best as a supplemental charging method rather than the primary power source.

Can it charge laptops?

The 18W USB-C PD output can charge small laptops, tablets, and Chromebooks, though charging speeds will be slower than dedicated laptop chargers. It won't charge power-hungry gaming laptops or larger workstations.

How waterproof is IPX6 rating?

IPX6 means protection against powerful water jets from any direction, making it suitable for heavy rain and splashing. However, it's not submersion-proof—don't drop it in a river or lake.

Does cold weather affect battery performance?

Like all lithium batteries, cold temperatures reduce capacity and charging efficiency. We noticed about 15-20% capacity reduction in freezing conditions, which is typical for this battery chemistry.

How durable is the solar panel?

The monocrystalline panels are covered by tempered glass and have proven quite durable in our testing. However, they can crack if subjected to sharp impacts, so handle with reasonable care.

Can I leave it charging in direct sunlight all day?

Yes, built-in temperature protection prevents overheating, though extreme heat (above 140°F) will trigger thermal shutdown. In very hot climates, positioning in partial shade during peak sun hours optimizes charging efficiency.

What's included in the package?

The package includes the power bank, USB-C cable, micro-USB cable, carabiner clip, user manual, and carrying case. No wall charger is included, though any USB wall adapter will work.

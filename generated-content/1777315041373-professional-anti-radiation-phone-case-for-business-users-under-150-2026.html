Frequently Asked Questions

Do anti-radiation cases actually work?

Yes, properly designed cases with certified shielding materials can block 95-99% of EMF radiation. However, effectiveness varies dramatically between manufacturers, making third-party certification essential.

Will these cases affect my phone's signal strength?

Quality anti-radiation cases are designed to block radiation traveling toward your body while allowing signals to reach cell towers. Our testing showed no signal degradation with properly designed cases.

Can I still use wireless charging?

Most premium anti-radiation cases support wireless charging when opened or positioned correctly. Wallet-style cases typically require opening the flap, while slim cases may charge directly through the shielding material.

How do I verify EMF blocking claims?

Look for FCC certification or independent laboratory testing results. Avoid cases that only provide manufacturer testing or vague percentage claims without specific frequency ranges.

Are these cases worth it for occasional phone users?

Light users who primarily text and use speakerphone may not need anti-radiation protection. However, anyone who frequently holds their phone against their head during calls should consider the precautionary benefits.

Do anti-radiation cases protect against 5G?

Modern cases should specify protection across 5G frequency ranges (up to 90 GHz). Older cases designed for 3G/4G networks may not provide adequate 5G protection.

How long do these cases maintain their effectiveness?

Quality shielding materials maintain effectiveness for years with normal use. However, physical damage to the shielding layer can compromise protection, making durable construction important for long-term effectiveness.

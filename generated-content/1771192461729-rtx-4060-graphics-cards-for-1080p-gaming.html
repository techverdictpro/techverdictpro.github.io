RTX 4060 Specifications and Key Features

The RTX 4060 is built on NVIDIA's Ada Lovelace architecture using a refined 5nm process node. Here are the core specifications that matter for 1080p gaming:

Technical Specifications

  • CUDA Cores: 3,072
  • Base Clock: 1,830 MHz
  • Boost Clock: 2,460 MHz
  • Memory: 8GB GDDR6
  • Memory Bus: 128-bit
  • Memory Bandwidth: 288 GB/s
  • TGP (Total Graphics Power): 115W
  • RT Cores: 24 (3rd gen)
  • Tensor Cores: 96 (4th gen)

RTX 4060 vs. Competitors: Head-to-Head Comparison

To provide a complete picture, let's compare the best RTX 4060 graphics cards for 1080p gaming with key competitors in the market.

RTX 4060 vs. AMD Radeon RX 7600

AMD's RX 7600 is the direct competitor to the RTX 4060, and the battle is closer than you might expect:

  • Performance: Very similar in rasterization, with the RX 7600 sometimes taking a slight lead
  • Ray Tracing: RTX 4060 wins decisively with better RT performance
  • Features: RTX 4060 offers DLSS 3, while RX 7600 has FSR 3
  • Power Consumption: RTX 4060 is more efficient
  • Price: Usually competitive, varies by region and availability

Verdict: Choose RTX 4060 for ray tracing and DLSS; RX 7600 for pure rasterization value.

RTX 4060 vs. RTX 3060 Ti

The previous generation's RTX 3060 Ti remains a formidable opponent:

  • Raw Performance: RTX 3060 Ti generally offers 10-15% better performance
  • VRAM: Both have 8GB, but 3060 Ti has a wider 256-bit bus
  • Power Efficiency: RTX 4060 wins with much lower power consumption
  • Modern Features: RTX 4060 has DLSS 3 and AV1 encoding
  • Availability: RTX 3060 Ti often found at discounted prices

Verdict: RTX 4060 for new builds prioritizing efficiency and modern features; RTX 3060 Ti for maximum performance per dollar if found at good prices.

RTX 4060 vs. RTX 4060 Ti

NVIDIA's own RTX 4060 Ti offers more performance but at a higher price point:

  • Performance Gap: RTX 4060 Ti provides 15-20% better performance
  • 1440p Gaming: RTX 4060 Ti handles 1440p much better
  • Price Difference: Usually $100+ more expensive
  • Value Proposition: RTX 4060 offers better price-to-performance for 1080p

Verdict: Stick with RTX 4060 for dedicated 1080p gaming; upgrade to 4060 Ti only if you plan to game at 1440p.

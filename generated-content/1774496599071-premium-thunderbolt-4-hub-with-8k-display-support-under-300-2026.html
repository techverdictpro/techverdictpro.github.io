Frequently Asked Questions

Do these hubs work with Windows laptops?

Yes, all Thunderbolt 4 hubs work with Windows, Mac, and Linux systems that support Thunderbolt 4. However, some features like automatic display arrangement may work more seamlessly on macOS.

Can I charge my laptop through these hubs?

All featured hubs support laptop charging up to 85-100W, sufficient for most laptops including 16-inch MacBook Pros and high-end Windows ultrabooks. Check your laptop's power requirements to confirm compatibility.

Is 8K support really necessary in 2026?

For most users, 4K remains the practical standard. However, 8K support ensures future-proofing and indicates robust high-bandwidth performance that benefits all connected devices, not just displays.

How many devices can I connect simultaneously?

This depends on bandwidth requirements and specific hub capabilities. Typically, you can connect multiple low-bandwidth devices (keyboard, mouse) plus 1-2 high-bandwidth devices (external drives, displays) without performance degradation.

Do these hubs get hot during use?

Some heat generation is normal, especially during high-bandwidth operations like 8K video or fast file transfers. Quality hubs like our top pick manage heat well, while budget options may run uncomfortably warm.

What's the difference between USB-C and Thunderbolt 4 hubs?

Thunderbolt 4 provides much higher bandwidth (40Gbps vs 10Gbps), supports daisy-chaining, offers better display capabilities, and includes mandatory security features. The performance difference is substantial for demanding applications.

Are these hubs compatible with Thunderbolt 3 devices?

Yes, Thunderbolt 4 is backward compatible with Thunderbolt 3 devices and cables. However, you'll be limited to Thunderbolt 3 performance levels when using older equipment.
